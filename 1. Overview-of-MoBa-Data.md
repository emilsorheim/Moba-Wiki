# Overview of MoBa Data

## What is MoBa?

The Norwegian Mother, Father and Child Cohort Study (MoBa) is a large population-based longitudinal health study conducted by the Norwegian Institute of Public Health (FHI).

- ~114,000 children
- ~95,000 mothers
- ~75,000 fathers
- Recruitment: 1999–2008
- Ongoing follow-up

MoBa is one of the largest cohort studies in the world.

---

## Purpose of the Study

MoBa aims to:

- Identify causes of diseases
- Study genetic and environmental risk factors
- Understand health development across the life course
- Support prevention and treatment research

---

## Types of Data

MoBa combines multiple data sources:

### 1. Questionnaire Data
- Self-reported information from participants
- Collected during pregnancy and throughout childhood
- Includes:
  - Health
  - Lifestyle
  - Diet
  - Psychosocial factors

---

### 2. Biological Data
- Blood samples (mother, father, child)
- Cord blood and urine
- Derived data:
  - Genomics (GWAS)
  - Biomarkers
  - Epigenetics

---

### 3. Registry Linkages
MoBa can be linked to national registries:

- Medical Birth Registry
- Patient Registry
- Prescription Registry

---

## Key Features

- Family-based (mother–father–child)
- Longitudinal (repeated measurements)
- Multi-source data
- National cohort
